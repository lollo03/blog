On October 25th, 2025, during [Linux Day Milan](https://linuxdaymilano.org/), I gave a talk on the GDPR and Chat Control.

Below you can find the abstract of the talk, along with the video and slides.

## Abstract

The EU promotes privacy through the GDPR and ethics through the AI Act. Yet, at the same time, it is pushing for Chat Control and the HLG recommendations, paving the way for the surveillance of conversations we currently consider private. This accessible, non-technical talk compares these contradictory policies. Are we truly strengthening digital rights, or are we building a European Big Brother?
